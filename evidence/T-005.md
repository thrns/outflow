## Implementation and filesystem coverage

Created `scripts/count_installed_bytes.py` and `scripts/tests/test_count_installed_bytes.py`. The JSON CLI omits the supplied path. The immutable Python result reports regular-file entry count/bytes, symbolic-link count/bytes, total logical bytes, limit bytes, and strict `below_limit` status. Hard-linked names are counted separately. Links are validated for an existing in-root target, measured by their own link-entry length, and never traversed; roots that are symlinks are rejected. Broken, mutually cyclic, and escaping links fail in a controlled way. Special entries fail rather than being assigned a guessed size.

The 27 new tests cover empty and zero-byte trees, single and nested files, mixed filenames, hidden and multiple entries, hard links, internal and root-cycle symlinks, mutually cyclic links, broken and outside-root links, missing/invalid/file/symlink roots, FIFO rejection, permission-denied directories, strict threshold boundaries without large files, deterministic repeated measurements, path-redacted JSON/errors, synthetic output, and no content/mtime/tree-entry changes. On this macOS host the symlink, hard-link, FIFO, and directory-permission tests executed and passed; no capability skips occurred.
